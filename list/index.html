Build notes

Buying from IKEA

  • Everything IKEA is on one order: both short frames, the 192 cm frame, the doors, hinges, rails, the gloss top panel and the sofa.
  • The BESTÅ 60×40×192 frame comes flat-packed in a box nearly 2 m long. Check it fits in the car, or add it to the sofa delivery.
  • The Cut My panel is made to order, so order it early.

Fire box

A third cabinet between the two BESTÅ, with the fire in its face. The body is cut from the 192 cm BESTÅ frame, so it is the same matt white as the cabinets. The face is cut from the gloss top panel, so it matches the SELSVIKEN doors.

Floating panel

  • Egger F800 Crystal Marble, 18 mm, cut to 1800 × 1100 and edged all round by Cut My. It weighs about 23 kg. Keep the corners square.
  • It hangs on a French cleat: rip one 2.4 m length of 22 × 100 timber at 45°. Screw one half through the slats into the wall with concrete screws, and glue and screw the other half to the back of the panel.
  • A 22 mm spacer at the bottom keeps it plumb. Everything sits 15 cm in from the panel's edges, so the panel stands 4.4 cm off the slats.
  • The TV mount goes on its own blocks into the wall, not on the panel.
  • Run the TV cable conduit before the slats go up.

Hanging the bookcases

  • The Homme Concept units are open-backed and made to stand on the floor, so hang them with brackets. Each one is a 3-cube unit with a 2-cube unit on top. Screw the two units together.
  • Fit two 40 mm angle brackets at the back of every cube, on the shelf where the books hide them. That is 10 brackets, or three 4-packs, per bookcase.
  • Screw each bracket down into the shelf with screws no longer than 10 mm, since the board is 12 mm. Screw it back through the 21 mm slats into the wall with plugs and 5 × 70 mm screws.
  • Each shelf then carries its own books straight into the wall. The brackets are silver; spray them white if they show.
